# CHAOS v0.36.4g — Contact Calibration QA

**Scope:** Focused surgical integration over v0.36.4f.

## Verified
- Original, dependency-free `src/contact_outcomes.js` recognizes **miss**, **light contact**, **meaningful contact**, **potential tackle**, and **guaranteed tackle** (only under a sustained and controlled *secured wrap* condition).
- A light, shallow arm brush remains a light contact; the lab's purposely wide miss remains a miss. Both are diagnostics, **not automatic tackle awards**.
- The controlled textbook-wrap lab preset progresses from no contact to meaningful contact and a qualified secured wrap once grip is established; it does **not** claim a tackle occurred in the isolated lab.
- Defensive-line torso capsule matches the modeled pads/torso breadth more closely; height/weight still affect proportions; all players retain 15 regions.
- Committed tackle dives can use direct anatomical contact in addition to existing proximity checks. A dive with no physical/swept contact is not a successful wrap based solely on circles overlapping.
- Standing tackles still primarily use the legacy solver to avoid a dangerous, uncalibrated replacement; valid pre-existing tackle outcomes are maintained.
- Live diagnostics expose category and related contact data through `GRIDIRON_DEBUG.debugContactCategory(carrier,defender)`.
- Automated unit tests: `node qa_v36_4g_contact.js`, `node qa_v36_4d_anatomy.js`, `node qa_v36_4e_contact.js`.
- Browser desktop/mobile: `python qa_v36_4g_browser.py`, `python qa_v36_4e_lab.py`, `python qa_v36_4f_angles.py`.
- Regression: safety/free kicks (`qa_v36_4b_rules.py`), kickoff end-zone choice (`qa_v36_4c_endzone_choice.py`), held airborne contest (`qa_v36_3b_air_contest.py`).
- `python build_standalone.py --check` passes.

## Known limitations / not claimed
- This is **not** a full rigid-body/ragdoll tackling solver, continuous physics solver, or comprehensive change to all standing tackles.
- A guaranteed-tackle category represents a tightly defined *already secured* wrap condition (actual contact, adequate grip, age, low relative speed), not a guarantee derived from a body overlap or player rating alone. User gameplay calibration is still required.
- Actual tackle animations, body-level momentum transfer and 11-vs-11 statistical balance still need extended visual testing.
- An existing `qa_v36_4_zone_iq.py` run was flaky/non-deterministic: it passed at desktop width but its defensive-front randomization produced no combo at mobile width; not counted as verified in this release. No Inside Zone targeting changes were made.
- No external code/assets/dependencies incorporated.
