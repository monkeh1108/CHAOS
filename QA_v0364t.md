# CHAOS v0.36.4t QA — Pursuit Reaction & Truck Feedback

## User reports / scope
- A kickoff coverage defender appeared to perfectly shadow a carrier's cuts at nearly zero distance while not visibly tackling.
- Truck actions were seemingly overpowering a defender, yet the low-contact subsystem displayed `STUMBLE` even when momentum was largely preserved.

## Implementation
1. `src/pursuit_reaction.js` applies a PUR/PRC/AGI-sensitive, ~0.095–0.215 second *observation interval* at within 5.3 yards, blending toward previously observed positions. This is aim only—no fake collision, speed correction, movement lock or automatic dive. Special-teams close-range pursuit now uses the same policy.
2. `src/contact_feedback_policy.js` ensures ordinary low-leg clips are not automatically called stumbles; low-foot forces, ankle contact, trips, and tackle thresholds are **unchanged**. A successful truck is reported as TRUCK when a real physical contact contest is won. A lost truck is TRUCK HELD. A truly tripped runner displays TRIPPED.
3. Kept previous conservative A, directional X, universal contact, hurdles and reengagement logic.

## Passing validations
- `python build_standalone.py --check`
- `node qa_v0364t_policy.js`: sampled close pursuit (no instantaneous cut following), eventual response, far pursuit unchanged.
- `node qa_v0364t_feedback.js`: truck cue priority, minor low-leg brush, significant stumble, genuine trip.
- `python qa_v0364s_browser.py`: desktop/mobile real-engine repeated broken-grip separation and later legal reengagement.
- `python qa_v0364r_universal.py`: real power collision with receiver without fake tackling.
- `python qa_v0364r_browser.py`: desktop/mobile conservative intent and directional X without target locking.
- `node qa_v0364s_reengagement.js`, `qa_v0364r_intelligence.js`, `qa_v0364q_low_contact.js`, `qa_v0364p_reactions.js`, `qa_v0364o_escape.js`.
- `python qa_v0364q_browser.py`: desktop/mobile low contact and hurdle conditions passed; high-impact trip assertion is **intermittent**, with some runs detecting a clip rather than a trip. It passed on a repeated run. The physical severity and trip thresholds were not modified by v0.36.4t.

## Honest limits
- This is not a complete new procedural foot planting or pursuit prediction model. Very close defenders may still look too close in some situations; test long kickoff returns and sharp alternating cuts.
- Feedback is based on one actual truck matchup versus a specific defender; two defenders hitting the runner together may need more refined composite event presentation in a future update.
- The high-impact low-contact browser scenario remains timing-sensitive and should be stabilized as a fixture separately from this targeted change.
