# CHAOS v0.36.4n — Diving Collision Integrity QA

## Purpose
Address the observed kickoff defender whose diving model crossed the runner's legs without any physical response. This is a **scoped, approximate swept low-body collision pass**, not a complete skeletal rigid-body solver and not an automatic tackle award.

## Implemented
- `src/dive_collision.js` detects moving defender forearms, hands, upper arms and torso against carrier thigh/shin/pelvis capsules, using pose-aware sweep sampling, including head/body-first contact diagnostics.
- A meaningful crossing contact stops the penetrating diver near first collision, transfers capped momentum and modest balance disruption, and reduces the defender's forward travel. It does not directly score a tackle. Very shallow brushes do not trigger a physical correction.
- `applyMotion` runs this pass for live ball carriers, including kickoffs, before legacy grip/tackle resolution. The retained standing collision solver remains unchanged.
- `finishMissedDefenderDive` unifies missed-dive grounded follow-through between scrimmage and special teams.
- Contact Lab includes a **Kickoff-speed low dive** repeatable scenario. `GRIDIRON_DEBUG.debugSetupLowDive` supports front, side, diagonal, and wide miss live setups; the evidence API includes recent low-dive contact information.

## Known limits
- This is sampled swept contact rather than an exact continuous collision solver. Extreme animation poses and crowds can still clip. Only low diving defender versus live ball carrier is handled by the new pass.
- Hitboxes and visual meshes are approximate; full per-joint momentum constraints, body-ground simulation, reliable jersey grip and angle-dependent knockdowns remain future work.
- A swept impact can occur with a failed tackle or a remaining successful legacy tackle; the two are intentionally distinct.
- Live browser cases use controlled replayed physical motion and a kickoff-speed lab case; **a complete human-played opening kickoff was not visually certified**. User feedback is still needed.
