# Gridiron Lab v0.34.4 — Focused gameplay patch

## Implemented

1. **Full end-zone depth:** players can move through the full 10-yard home end zone. The player physics clamp has been expanded from 7–112 to the full 0–120 coordinate field. Existing goal-line safety and touchdown rulings remain.
2. **QB sacks:** before the QB releases a pass, defensive contact now enters the existing tackle/grip system, including rating, leverage, and release checks. A secured tackle ends the down as a sack; a sack behind the offensive goal line scores a safety.
3. **Pass-flight switching:** B now changes to a nearby eligible defender when the ball is in flight, prioritizing the intended receiver or catch location rather than the quarterback. Pre-snap positional cycling remains unchanged.
4. **CPU-owned snaps:** during ordinary away-team offensive possessions, A and the onscreen snap control cannot trigger the snap. The CPU snaps after a varying pre-snap interval (approximately 4–9 seconds after the 20-second clock starts). Defensive audibles and pre-snap switching are still available.

## Deliberately postponed

- Live kickoff bounce, loose-ball pickup, and removal of returner teleport.
- Smarter CPU pre-snap reads and audible decisions based on observed defensive alignment. This patch adds the pause only, not AI reads.
- Expanded D-line rush moves, diving animations, and pass leading.
