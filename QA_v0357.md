# Gridiron Lab v0.35.7 QA — 11 November 2026

## Scope
- Temporary **60-second quarters**, defined by a single `QUARTER_DURATION_SECONDS` setting. The two-minute warning is disabled while quarters are only one minute.
- Separate `src/protection_read.js` module: reads only visible defensive alignment, not hidden defensive play choices.
- A center/near-side lineman can identify a *possible* additional rusher; the RB can pick up the recognized threat when it actually charges the pocket. Actual existing rusher assignments remain prioritized.
- A read can fail or be a bluff: no guaranteed recognition or protection.

## Limitations
- Full human Xbox-controller testing and multi-possession visual evaluation not performed.
- This is first-pass, probabilistic blitz anticipation, not an advanced line-shift/hot-protection system. It deliberately does not identify the hidden called blitz.
- The older `qa_v31_game_flow.py` expects 900-second quarters; its original duration assertion is intentionally obsolete while the temporary 60-second setting is enabled.
- Future game settings can expose configurable quarter length, accelerated clock, injuries, etc.; not yet implemented.
