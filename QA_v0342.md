# Gridiron Lab v0.34.2 — targeted gameplay stability

Changes from v0.34.1 (only src/engine.js):
- Removed the 18-second return auto-whistle. Legitimate tackle, score, boundary, and fair-catch events remain.
- Returners now retain fatigue after the whistle; resting players recover between plays.
- Reduced idle in-play stamina regeneration, and set between-play recovery to approximately four non-carrying plays from near exhaustion, with very little recovery for repeated carries.
- On returns, coverage defenders receive a short blocker bypass after a broken engagement, reducing immediate reattachment.
- CPU running backs detect low forward progress near multiple defenders and seek a less congested side.
- Added a forward-progress-stopped whistle after sustained near-stationary contact with at least two defenders, rather than a timer on every play.

Automated passes: standalone source match, JS syntax, policy unit, audible/controller (1120/390), kickoff/punt (1120/390), game flow, 144 formation matrix, pre-snap (1120/390), QB/rush controls (1120/390), protection switching (1120/390), airborne catch controls.

Limitations: physical Xbox controller and long live-play scenario balance remain untested. The new congestion behavior is deliberately conservative and needs human gameplay evaluation. The current version does not yet implement the broader adaptive CPU or coverage tuning.
