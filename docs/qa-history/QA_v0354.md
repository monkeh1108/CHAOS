# Gridiron Lab v0.35.4 — Defensive Pre-Snap Positioning QA

## Scope
Surgical update from v0.35.3: pre-snap defender movement, visible assignments in 2D/3D, and neutral-stick AI resuming assignments after the snap. No tackle, stamina, kickoff, throw physics, or hit-stick changes.

## Changes
- New `src/defensive_presnap.js` pure positioning policy; left stick, keyboard WASD/arrows, or mobile joystick move the selected defender prior to the snap.
- Defender stays on the defense's side of the line and inside the sidelines, with a basic teammate-overlap guard.
- Man coverage retains the same receiver, zone coverage keeps its area identity with bounded shade changes, and run-fit responsibilities remain assigned. Movement changes the player's starting physical location; no snap-time teleport.
- Defensive assignment feedback is visible for the selected defender in the 3D field and on the 2D fallback; the on-screen status names the assignment.
- During normal CPU offensive drives, the defensive call sheet no longer covers the field by default. VIEW still opens it; X still opens audibles and B switches defenders.
- When user steering is released after the snap, the selected defender once again reads and executes his actual assignment; moving the stick immediately takes manual control.
- Existing offensive route art remains hidden from the human defense.
- CPU still handles its own snap; user cannot trigger it.

## Verified
- Standalone build matches the maintained source (`python build_standalone.py --check`); JS syntax checks pass.
- v0.35.4 browser test at 1120px and 390px: move defender, zone shading update, keep assignment at snap, legal-field bounds, no stamina drain, switch defenders, audible prevents movement, and CPU snap.
- Manual 3D visual smoke under a virtual display: WebGL initialized, on-field assignment graphic rendered, no browser exceptions.
- v0.33 controller and rush policy; v0.34.1 audibles; v0.34.4 end-zone/sack/air-switch; v0.35.0 deep anticipation; v0.35.2 run pursuit; v0.35.3 interception; v0.31 game flow; and 144 formation combinations passed in targeted runs.

## Not tested or limited
- Physical Xbox controller and long sessions were not tested. Browser tests simulate inputs and game state.
- Previous older kickoff/return regression caveats remain; this update does not claim to fix them.
- Formation-shift audibles (same personnel) and advanced animation/graphics are separate future work.
- Defensive coverage effectiveness after unusually extreme pre-snap repositioning needs hands-on testing; the system preserves assignments but will not prevent strategic mistakes caused by moving out of position.

## Manual test
1. Start a game and reach a CPU offensive drive. The field should be visible without automatically covering it with a call sheet.
2. Press B to cycle through defenders. Move a safety and a corner with the left stick. Watch their assignment indicators and the bottom status description.
3. Tap X for a defensive audible and confirm movement stops while selecting; close it and move again.
4. Let the CPU snap. Release the stick and see whether the player follows his zone, man, run-fit, or rush assignment from the moved position. Use the stick to steer him manually at any time.
5. Confirm offense, audibles, clock, kickoffs, catches, and tackles still work as before.
