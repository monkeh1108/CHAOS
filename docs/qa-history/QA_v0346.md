# GridIron Lab v0.34.6 — focused return coverage and tackle reliability

## Changes
- Return coverage now tracks real movement during pursuit. A defender that remains effectively stationary for over 0.9 seconds while over 7 yards from the returner releases a stale block and attempts a timed lateral bypass. This does not teleport the player or change their speed rating.
- Trailing tackle contacts no longer automatically break solely because the grip-security ceiling is low after approximately 0.73 seconds. Actual escapes remain possible based on grip quality, runner ratings, and movement.
- Existing audibles, stamina model, blocking, kickoff landing behavior, CPU cadence, scoring, and offensive logic are otherwise unchanged.

## Limitations
- The user's exact stuck far-left kickoff player after an actual block has not yet been reproduced end-to-end by the new targeted test. The stationary-player recovery change is a candidate fix, not a guaranteed resolution.
- The repeated hunching/chasing during full-speed live returns still requires user gameplay testing and may have additional causes (collision, pursuit angles, animation states).
- Physical Xbox controller testing not performed.
- Ball teleport to kickoff landing spot remains intentionally unchanged; physical bounces/loose-ball recoveries are a separate feature.
