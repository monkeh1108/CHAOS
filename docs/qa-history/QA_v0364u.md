# CHAOS v0.36.4u — contact eligibility, temporary cues, returner role handoff

## Changes
- Advance action cues during special-teams plays, preventing BREAK TACKLE feedback from remaining onscreen indefinitely.
- Permit a physically recovered defender to reengage a near-stationary carrier after sustained contact; moving carriers still require real separation after a broken grip.
- Permit genuine overlapping body contact to qualify for tackle evaluation despite an out-of-date facing direction. Actual anatomical collision/contact assessment still determines whether a grip can establish.
- When the alternate deep returner fields a kick, swap the designated escort role to the former primary returner. Use the actual catcher for the rest of that update frame.
- Give trailing deep-return escorts an achievable diagonal catch-up path instead of incorrectly chasing forward threats while far behind the ball carrier.
- Rename the confusing TRUCK HELD feedback to TRUCK STOPPED. Debug cues remain temporary test information, not intended final gameplay UI.

## Limitations / next field tests
- The new trailing-returner policy is deliberately not a speed boost: a far-behind returner may not reach a fast carrier before the play ends.
- The near-stationary repeated-contact rule has unit-level verification; field-test whether tightly clustered defenders can now tackle a stopped returner without false/magnetic wraps.
- Temporary BREAK TACKLE/TRUCK labels remain visible for diagnosis. Final presentation should come from animation and physics, with cues disabled outside optional debug instrumentation.
- Long-term ball flight/spin and animated midair recovery remain separate future milestones.
