# Gridiron Lab v0.35.8 — User-blitzing safety QB contact

## Root cause
- In `src/engine.js`, `resolveTackles()` previously skipped all non-rush-assigned defenders against a quarterback holding the ball.
- Later in the same frame, `update()` repeatedly put all non-rushers into a 0.2s tackle cooldown. A user-controlled safety rushing the QB could not establish a tackle, even after physical contact.
- CPU QB pressure detection also only checked originally designated rushers; visibly charging defenders outside the original rush plan were ignored.

## Surgical changes
- Permit any defender who physically reaches the QB while he is holding the football to enter normal tackle resolution.
- Remove redundant pre-throw tackle check and the forced non-rusher cooldown.
- Include nearby defenders visibly charging the pocket in the CPU QB's pressure assessment, without reading the hidden defensive assignment.
- No change to defensive-memory learning, play selection, protection assignment rules, kicking, stamina or animation.

## Scope limitation
The v0.35.7 pre-snap protection AI makes a single rating-dependent visible-alignment read per snap. It does not yet develop cross-snap offensive protection memory in response to repeated user blitzes; that is a separate future feature. The current patch repairs the inability to register user safety/linebacker sacks and improves physical pressure awareness, not all repeated-blitz adaptation.
