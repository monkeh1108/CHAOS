# CHAOS v0.36.4r QA — Defensive Intelligence & Contact Commitment

## Changes
- Added separate `src/tackle_intelligence.js` for deterministic angle/closing/position evaluation and directional physical-commitment policy. Decisions do not create hitbox contact or tackle outcomes.
- A (Xbox) / Z (keyboard) is conservative *intent* and may be held before contact without activating a tackle animation or grip. Normal collision remains necessary. An established conservative engagement receives the existing conservative grip bonus.
- X (Xbox / keyboard) commits to current stick, motion or facing direction, not an auto-targeted ball carrier. Nearby physical player -> power hit; at distance -> committed dive; no opponent -> miss and normal dive recovery.
- Right-stick up power commitment also uses direction rather than targeting. A real collision with an offensive receiver, blocker, or lineman transfers force and balance loss, without awarding an automatic tackle or guaranteed pancake.
- CPU contact intentions consider gap, facing, closing speed, pursuit and ratings; lower dives are committed *before* contact when an escaping runner is plausibly reachable. Contact still decides the outcome; no dice-roll low-tackle selection.
- Corrected committed-dive recovery cooldown from discarding genuine acquired tackle grips. Updated overlap-sensitive arm presentation so an A hold alone does not create arms-out phantom wraps.
- Existing collisions, blocking engagement, escape mechanics, low-foot contact, hopping, and pass-defense A/X/Y remain in place.

## Known limits / next iteration
- This is procedural 2D contact + 3D presentation, not a full skeletal rigid-body solver. Hit-stick penetration resolution, blocker deflection and pancake outcomes still need player field testing.
- Automated tests verify several controlled contact scenarios, not every possible 22-player animation path or NFL penalty.
- CPU defensive selection is a first geometry-based implementation; it needs in-game balance testing for dive frequency, rushing decisions and conservative tackling.
- Existing non-ballcarrier dive fall reactions rely on the shared body-contact/ground-recovery solver; specific special-case animations can be improved later.
