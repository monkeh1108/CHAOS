# CHAOS v0.36.4j — QA and limitations

## Scope
Standing defender raises arms naturally in anticipation of contact, gradually approaching 90°. Diving defender gradually raises both arms from lower position into the existing 135° reach. No grip until engagement; no alterations to tackle success.

## Limits
This is an approximation with a procedural 3D rig, not biomechanical hand IK or complete wrap-up animations. Forearm positioning is gradually blended; player contact detection remains distinct from guaranteed tackle completion. The live wrap pre-reach currently reacts to distance/facing, not predictive trajectory or individual reach ratings. Actual on-field visual review by the user is needed.
