# Gridiron Lab v0.34.8 — kickoff fall recovery and rear tackles

## Findings and changes
- Root cause found: `stepSpecial()` did not call `tickGround()`, unlike normal scrimmage `update()`. A player knocked down on a kickoff could remain in `ground` state forever. Special-teams now advances fall/recovery states every simulation tick and prevents grounded players from issuing normal pursuit commands until recovery.
- Rear-contact tackling: a maintained wrap from immediately behind the ball carrier can now complete a physical drag-down after 0.56 seconds with a secured grip of 0.65, using existing tackle initiation and escape logic rather than a guaranteed instant tackle.
- Successful tackle escapes now briefly knock the failed defender down with an explicit broken-tackle state, so the break is legible and has a physical cost.
