# v0.34.5 targeted return/pursuit + stamina pass

Changes from v0.34.4:
- While controlling kickoff coverage on defense, an idle selected defender resumes AI pursuit until the player steers him (addresses the far-left stationary controlled defender).
- Kicker enters normal rating-based coverage pursuit after ball is fielded.
- Pursuit defenders retain close-range effort to reach tackle distance; no guaranteed catch-up or tackles.
- Reduced continuous sprint stamina drain, increased contact stamina cost modestly. Three-play between-snap recovery remains unchanged.

Not changed: kick landing teleport and loose-ball physics, defensive coverage, route-collision AI, pre-snap player movement, adaptive calls, scoring. These need separate patches and tests.
