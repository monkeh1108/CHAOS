# CHAOS v0.36.4v — Tackle & Pile Contact Repair

## Reproduced defect
Using the playable v0.36.4u engine in a browser with a ball carrier initially at rest and six or eleven rear-approaching defenders, the carrier escaped the entire crowd and reached the end zone. Tackle links repeatedly failed to remain established despite close 2D collision impulses. This was reproducible in a controlled **moving-after-contact** scenario; it does not exactly replay the user's live input or all 22 players.

## Root cause identified
The collision solver's body separation distance for an ungripped carrier/defender pair could exceed the center-to-center distance where the anatomical torso/arm capsules registered qualifying contact. Four Gauss-Seidel passes moved the players apart before `resolveTackles` evaluated the anatomical overlap. Thus body-to-body pushing could occur while wrap formation saw a `miss` or `light contact`. Repeated pushes without established grips kept the gang-tackle/pile solver from activating. This explains the observed symptom but does not prove every aspect of the reported live play had a single cause.

## Surgical repair
- Adjust non-engaged carrier/defender collision **core separation** by 0.17 yd to let meaningful shoulder/hip geometry physically meet. Keep the original collision broadphase for square-impact and high-speed hit detection.
- Record actual torso/arm body-region evidence at the first collision pass, before positional separation, for the current physics step only.
- Let the tackle solver use stronger verified pre-separation anatomical evidence where post-separation sampling has lost the contact. Bare proximity and fingertip brushes still do not award a grip. Eligibility, recovery, grip progression, tackle breaks, and finish thresholds are unchanged.
- No artificial tackle roll, forced knockdown, pile-speed multiplier, or new HUD cue.

## Still needs human gameplay testing
This controlled scenario doesn't establish that every kickoff return, full 22-player crowd, or stationary-input pile behaves perfectly. In particular, live diving contact and lineman pile-support timing may exhibit other edge cases. Test standing still while coverage arrives from behind, then running into a crowd and testing strong runner drag-throughs. Keep future visual cue cleanup and broad AI overhaul separate.
