## Limitations
- Long game-flow test timed out in this execution, so the full flow suite is **not verified** here.
- Browser tests cannot establish subjective blocking appearance, realistic hand contacts, or physical-controller behavior. Both should be tested in live gameplay.
- Stances are simplified procedural low-poly poses, not detailed skeletal rigs; the one-hand ground contact is approximate.
- No claims that every kickoff block or every strength mismatch is perfectly tuned.
