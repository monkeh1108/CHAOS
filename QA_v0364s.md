# CHAOS v0.36.4s — Same-Defender Reattachment Hotfix

## Reported playtest behavior
A defender remained attached to a ball carrier, lost a grip repeatedly, immediately closed the tiny gap, then generated five or six separate BREAK TACKLE notifications over a long run.

## Root-cause finding
The previous release path removed the tackle link and assigned only a short reaction cooldown. Once the cooldown expired, the same defender could open another link on near-overlap without proving actual post-release separation. The weaker-grip escape threshold also allows a series of break-tackle results when such links repeatedly restart. No tackle-rating nerf has been applied in this hotfix.

## Targeted correction
- Added `src/tackle_reengagement.js` as a standalone policy independent of collision and tackle outcome physics.
- On an **actual escape**, record the former carrier and require genuine physical separation (at least the combined body radii plus a small balance-sensitive margin), regained footing, and recovery from stun/dive cooldown before the same defender may establish another grip.
- Defender may still chase immediately, and a legitimate later second tackle is allowed. This doesn't force a fall, magnetize anyone, or grant a tackle.
- `getState()` exposes `players[].reengage` for diagnosis, plus a narrowly scoped debug test hook.
- No changes to player ratings, break-tackle odds, grip strength equations, blocking, hurdling, or run pursuit.

## QA verified

- `python qa_v0364s_browser.py`: real engine Chromium desktop/mobile, actual link release, overlapping re-grip prohibited, later truly separated re-engagement permitted.
- `python qa_v0364r_browser.py` and `python qa_v0364r_universal.py`: A/X and non-ballcarrier hit regression.
- `node qa_v0364o_escape.js`, `qa_v0364p_reactions.js`, `qa_v0364q_low_contact.js`, `qa_v0364r_intelligence.js`, `qa_v36_4k_grip.js`: all passed.
- `python build_standalone.py --check`: passed.

## Known limits
This specifically fixes the immediate re-latch loop, not the entire balance of powerful runner-vs-defender matchups. Multiple *legitimate* tackles by a chasing defender can still be broken, and outcomes still warrant user playtesting. The existing 2D body-solver with 3D visual presentation remains unchanged.
