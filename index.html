Controls

WASD or arrow keys steer: in 3D, W / ↑ runs downfield, A / ← and D / → move left/right on screen. In the original 2D view, D / → runs downfield and W / ↑ moves toward the upper sideline. Hold Shift to sprint; Space jukes, T trucks, G/Y hurdles while carrying the ball, and F hold for stiff-arm after the handoff. On keyboard, V spins, C protects the football, X tap gives up, X hold dives, Q is an impact block in O-line mode, Z is a conservative defensive tackle, X is a contextual hit/dive, and H/N are high/low right-stick equivalents in defense mode, and B switches defenders. When the ball is airborne, watch the yellow ground catch-zone ring and the white moving ball shadow; the possessed ball carrier has an amber ring and your selected player a cyan ring. Players react to the landing area automatically if you do not steer them. Press B on Xbox or keyboard B (or tap SWITCH RECEIVER) to manually steer the intended receiver into the catch point, then choose X/A/Y. Once you steer the ball carrier, you keep control even when you release the keys; use GIVE AI CONTROL to turn the auto-runner back on. On pass plays, tap an X/Y/A/B icon or briefly press keys 1–4 for a lob; hold longer for a touch pass and a full hold for a bullet. The QB will automatically lead the receiver based on his route and passing accuracy; coverage and pressure still matter. After the throw: The chosen catch or defensive ball-play style appears above the player; high-point jumps, swats, and defensive interception attempts now use different motion silhouettes. Xbox X selects a RAC catch (or defender swat), A selects possession catch (or play the receiver), and Y selects aggressive catch (or ball hawk / interception attempt). Keyboard J/K/L select those three options. Defensive actions work only when your selected defender is in position. The game will choose context-appropriate actions for computer-controlled players. In DEFENSE mode, the CPU QB uses VIS (field vision) and DEC (decision speed) to evaluate routes, checkdowns, pressure, and scramble opportunities. On phones, tap an icon or the bottom receiver bar. If keys do not respond, click the field to focus it. In the playbook, use the Xbox D-pad to navigate, A to choose, LB/RB to cycle offensive formations or defensive fronts, X to snap, and VIEW to show/hide the sheet. Game Day mode now uses four 15-minute quarters, a 40-/25-second play clock, 3 timeouts per half and a play-calling overlay. On a punt, press Y (Xbox) or K (keyboard), or tap FAIR CATCH while the ball is airborne; otherwise steer the returner. Kickoffs use an on-field kicker; kick destination is based on KPW/KAC ratings. When receiving, choose RETURN LEFT, MIDDLE or RIGHT to direct blocking. Field goals and PATs use a preliminary rating/accuracy model. No artificial 16-second post-snap expiration. Use SAVE PLAY TRACE after any snap to download the exact player movements, pursuit intents, stamina, and controls at 10Hz; you can share the JSON file for analysis of a play that felt wrong. No screen recording is performed.

Physics tuning room

Editable athletes

These sliders affect the simulation, not merely visual animations. QB: VIS = field vision, DEC = decisiveness, TOR = throw on run, BSK = pocket escape / sack-breaking tendency. Ball carrier: JUK = lateral cut technique and momentum retention, SFA = stiff-arm execution. WRs: RTE route technique, CUT plant sharpness, DBL double-move sell, REL press release, HND hand fighting. CBs: PRS press technique, JAM physical jam, MIR mirrored footwork, RCR route recognition, DIS discipline against double moves. Run PRC is separate from pass-route RCR. These interact as individual matchups and do not guarantee separation. PRC controls run recognition delay; PUR controls pursuit interception and run angles; STA controls stamina and sprint duration. HIT = impact tackling, PMV = power moves against blocks, and IBL = impact-block technique. The engine chooses techniques according to contact speed, angle and ratings. Body-to-body hits now transfer momentum through chains. A standing gang tackle can become a moving pile: linemen arrive from behind, lock in, and drive with ratings-based force. Stylized overhead arm/leg motions reflect contact (not motion-capture animation). Try lowering the MLB PRC or PUR, or RB STA, then run Power Left again.

Play-by-play

Live physics

v0.33 adds A conservative and X proximity-dependent hit/dive defensive tackles, D-pad playbook navigation, slower CPU route reads, and live QB-rollout awareness for pass rushers. v0.32 expands Game Day with return Left/Middle/Right, on-field kicker, kicker-controlled kick placement, possession-aware offense/defense role changes, and CPU opponent play calls. v0.31 adds four-quarter scoreboard, timed play calling, six-point touchdowns and tries, kickoff and punt returns, a field-goal option, timeouts, and a new play-call sheet. v0.30 adds distinct jump / dive / ball-skill animations, Y-button hurdling on the ground, and above-player catch/swat confirmation labels. v0.29 adds projected catch-point rings, moving airborne-ball shadow, possession/control rings, automatic receiver catch-point pursuit, and cross-field defensive-back/safety support on deep throws. v0.28.1 fixes pass-protection assignments and adds B-to-receiver switching on airborne passes. v0.28 adds QB spies, contain and blitz/stunt plans, more formations and concepts, RAC/possession/aggressive catch decisions and swat/ball-hawk/play-receiver interactions. v0.24 adds rating-driven route and coverage variation, auto mixed defensive calls, smoother high-CUT jukes, and hold-A stiff-arm animations. v0.23.1 fixes movement orientation for the behind-offense 3D camera while preserving 2D controls. v0.23 adds a camera behind the offense, ball-following movement, a wider passing view, field-level 3D play art, correctly placed back-of-end-zone goalposts, more deliberate defensive stamina use, and field-aligned repeated jukes. v0.22 introduces a first low-poly WebGL 3D view, state-driven player poses and CPU quarterback decision-making. v0.21 adds non-stop routes, QB-scramble legality and threat-based blocking; v0.20 corrected touchdown lengths and post-catch WR blocking, with retained sharper 2-yard lateral jukes, retained v0.17 corner-lane blocking and v0.11 defensive intelligence: Off-ball players hit hard enough can fall, catch their breath and recover before rejoining the same live play. Collisions and ratings determine thresholds; this is not a knockout or medical injury system. Individual gap assignments, delayed recognition of visible guard and running-back cues, force/contain, cutback fits, last-line safety angles and velocity-based interception. PRC, PUR and STA are editable and affect behavior; sprinting is now limited by consequential stamina drain. Previous contact and controller improvements remain intact. Earlier contact balance: Responsive agility-weighted foot plants, AI sprinting, momentum hit sticks, impact blocks and power rush moves supplement the domino collisions. Defender TAK/HIT versus carrier TRK/STR and help from additional tacklers determine contact outcomes. Domino hits transfer momentum player-to-player. Standing piles let linemen push against defenders; players churn legs and lean on contact. Results are calculated by weight, relative velocity, blocking/strength, angle and tackle grip. Xbox controls are retained. Existing controller support: Once the handoff or catch occurs, defenders pursue the runner; receivers, linemen and backs identify blocking threats; the quarterback trails safely. A broken tackle keeps the play live, and the whistle ends all movement. Automatic runners never pursue a waypoint behind them, manual steering stays engaged after releasing a key, and plays no longer expire after 16 seconds. Prototype scope: a top-down 11-on-11 skill-and-contact laboratory, not a finished NFL simulation. Speeds, mass, momentum, turning limits, release trajectories and catch reach are modeled; line play and tackling use tunable approximations, not full rigid-body anatomy. No official Madden assets or NFL licenses.
