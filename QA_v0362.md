# Gridiron Lab v0.36.2 — Blocking Arms and Close Pursuit

## Scope
A presentation-side correction for engaged blocking arms and a narrow close-range pursuit steering change. No changes to block outcomes, tackle probability, stamina, audibles, game flow, or player ratings.

## Findings
- Previous blocking rotations angled both arms backward, giving a forward-hunched / 'Naruto run' silhouette even during live blocks.
- The presentation policy considered any 'rush' intent a braced/contact state. Now only actual block links trigger the dedicated hands-on-block pose.
- Close-range pursuit could preserve too much lateral motion as the defender converged on a runner, producing short circles. Close-contact lateral damping is now stronger without changing acceleration caps or speed ratings.
