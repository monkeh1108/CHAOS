# v0.35.1 — Defensive memory only

Tracks completed scrimmage plays, target receiver, air-yard depth, and QB runs; excludes kickoffs and penalties before snap. Records are descriptive only and do not influence defense. Neutral 2/2 prior avoids 100% certainty after four same-type plays. Resets on new game.

## Known limits
- Tendency estimates are descriptive percentages with a neutral prior and a cap; they are NOT defensive AI behavior.
- Classifies deep/intermediate/short from measured throw distance, not secret intended route; no target or air-yard depth is recorded for passes never thrown.
- Field position and game context, sacks as distinct tags, and real-time route recognition remain later steps.
- Physical Xbox controller gameplay and multiple full live games not tested here.
