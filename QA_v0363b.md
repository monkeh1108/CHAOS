# Gridiron Lab v0.36.3b — Deep-ball contest eligibility and queued input

## Changes

1. **Role-aware, forward-looking eligibility:** `src/air_contest_policy.js` evaluates the ball's intended catch point and remaining flight time, defender top speed/current movement, reach and whether the defender is in coverage of the targeted receiver. A defender who is running with the target can queue a swat/play-receiver/ball-hawk decision while still far from the landing spot; he is not forced to wait for a fixed five-yard bubble.
2. **Help defenders must read the ball:** a non-paired defender becomes a candidate only after his flight-reading delay, and only if a realistic travel budget makes the landing area attainable. Unrelated defenders far downfield cannot queue remote interception or swat actions.
3. **Held Y and A:** Xbox Y/A held before becoming eligible are polled during the flight and applied once eligibility is reached. The choice is not repeated each frame. Previously pressing Y outside the old zone was silently discarded even if the player ran into position. X remains an open-field committed dive if outside the eligible passing play, and becomes a swat only if eligible.
4. **Queue is not an animation or catch:** early selections save player intent, with a small HUD cue. They do not trigger a distant jump. The actual attempt pose now begins only near the catch area; existing live-ball reach, ball height, timing, ratings and interception/breakup probabilities are unchanged.
5. **Presentation:** the HUD says **ON THE PLAY** or **NOT IN CONTEST**, rather than implying the larger anticipatory zone grants physical catch range. Keyboard and pointer actions still use the same eligibility check.

## Scope and limitations

- The new policy is a **prototype estimate**, not a full human perceptual model or real-world flight simulator. User playtesting still determines how deep balls feel with a controller.
- Y does not guarantee an interception; the existing physics and rating checks still decide whether the defender reaches and secures the ball.
- A held Xbox Y/A queues once in an eligible situation, but keyboard keys work through their existing press-selection bindings (not a new keyboard hold-buffer feature).
- No changes to general tackling, blocks, Mesh, formations, stamina or existing probability balance.
- **Future design, not in this build:** physically simulated, varied 50/50 ball fights and secure-through-contact ratings: receiver **Catch Grip (CGR)** and defender **Ball Disruption (BDR)**; distinguish incomplete catches from completed-catch fumbles under football possession rules. No new rated attributes or hand-fighting animations are claimed in v0.36.3b.
