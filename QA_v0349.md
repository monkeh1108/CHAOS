# Gridiron Lab v0.34.9 — Deep Coverage and Mesh Awareness

## Changes
- Deep-zone defenders prioritize downfield threats in their assigned lane over short receivers, and now can carry routes beyond the former 40-yard movement cap.
- Man defenders keep a little more depth against clearly vertical receivers; existing route-reaction delays and ratings remain in place.
- Mesh route runners apply small opposite-direction traffic avoidance steering when approaching a teammate. Their underlying route waypoints stay intact.
- New `src/coverage_route_policy.js` encapsulates the decisions; `build_standalone.py` includes it in generated HTML.
- No intentional changes to tackling, kickoffs, audibles, stamina, clock or control mappings.
