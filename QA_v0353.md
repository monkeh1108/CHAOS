# Gridiron Lab v0.35.3 — Interception Spot Fix QA

## Fixed
- `src/engine.js` `awardAirInterception` and `awardAirCatch` now hand `finish('intercept', ...)` the interceptor's location, not `playStart`.
- `finish()` converts the catch spot correctly for the opposite possession; end-zone catches are touchbacks at own 20.
- Overlay/logging show the new possession spot instead of misleading offensive yardage.
- Debug-only `GRIDIRON_DEBUG.debugSecureInterception` allows isolated regression of the two existing real interception award paths.

## Limits
- The engine still ends plays immediately on interception, so interception return yards are not simulated and the new drive starts where the defender secured possession, not where they would be tackled on a hypothetical return.
- Extended user gameplay, physical Xbox controller and standalone visual rendering quality were not validated in this patch.
- No REC rating or stamina adjustments were implemented; it is the next optional balance step.
- This patch did not resolve the historical older kickoff test's intermittent stall from the previous release; the dedicated kickoff recovery test listed above passed.
