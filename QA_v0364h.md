## Deliberately unchanged
- Tackle probability, collision-category thresholds, block physics, movement and defensive AI.
- Existing Contact Lab scenarios and gameplay flow.
- No new third-party libraries or models.

## Limitations / manual follow-up
- Procedural, simplified 3D arms; not yet detailed IK hand placement around independently moving thighs.
- Arm articulation in the renderer and narrow-phase anatomy uses shared angular inputs, but capsule-based shapes remain approximations of the visual meshes.
- Actual real-match diving-leg-wrap appearance, especially late contact and body fall transitions, still needs direct gameplay validation.
- Contact does not guarantee a tackle; secure grip remains required.
